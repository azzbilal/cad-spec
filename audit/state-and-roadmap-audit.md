### A3. Leakage, independence, and memorization

**Fact, high:** replication has zero overlap with train, dev, and test in full five-parameter tuples, exact full prompts in each tier, exact rev A programs, rev A parameter tuples, and either direction of target-versus-source parameter matching. This is genuine instance disjointness. [leakage.json:14](C:/Users/bgare/cad-spec-audit-out/scratch/leakage.json:14).

**It is not structural disjointness.** Every replication change-field combination appears in training. Replication uses 14 combinations; training uses all 15 one/two-field combinations. Number-normalized L3 text has zero overlap with training templates but shares the two evaluation wordings with dev and test. L1/L2 skeletons are shared, and 14 normalized L4 prompt forms overlap training. Every replication thickness, diameter, and margin value appears individually in training; length does so in 25/60 and width in 32/60. [leakage.json:27](C:/Users/bgare/cad-spec-audit-out/scratch/leakage.json:27) [leakage.json:76](C:/Users/bgare/cad-spec-audit-out/scratch/leakage.json:76) [leakage.json:224](C:/Users/bgare/cad-spec-audit-out/scratch/leakage.json:224).

Nearest-train distance for each replication spec is archived without exposing its parameter vector. The metric is Euclidean distance after dividing differences by generator ranges [180,130,11,8.5,20] mm. Across the 60 nearest neighbors: minimum 0.07837, median 0.24269, maximum 0.36642. None is within all scorer field tolerances simultaneously. Equal-coordinate counts for the nearest neighbor are 0/1/2/3 coordinates for 35/21/3/1 specs. This metric describes numerical proximity, not semantic novelty. [leakage.json:143](C:/Users/bgare/cad-spec-audit-out/scratch/leakage.json:143).

**Inference, high:** memorizing target tuples is not supported by these overlap checks. Learning or memorizing a template, five-field extraction, and the formula pitch = size - 2 margin could explain the result. That is useful learning for this generator; it is not evidence of broad engineering generalization.

To make that limitation concrete, I authored an L4 parser from the public grammar. It extracts rev A dimensions and pitch, recovers the original margin from both axes, applies the ordered field edits, and recomputes both pitches. The solver never receives the target spec. It passed train 200/200 and dev 30/30; the script hash was frozen before local test/replication evaluation; it then passed 60/60 on each. There was no evaluation-driven revision. ### P1. Repair the reward contract and release scorer 0.5

**Mechanism:** first decide whether the family allows fillets, chamfers and pockets. For a strict rectangular four-through-hole plate, enforce that geometry explicitly. Keep the inclusive convention with a separate small numerical epsilon. Use smaller specialized geometry tolerances for residual material and open bores; do not reuse a 3% volume allowance as a feature whitelist. For continuous/off-grid future tasks, define allowed precision explicitly rather than relying on “next grid point.”
